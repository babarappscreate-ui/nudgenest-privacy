NUDGENEST

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Overview

NudgeNest is a private, offline productivity app. It does not require an account, display advertising, use analytics, or send checklist data to the developer or any third party.

Information stored locally

Nest names, checklist items, categories, pin status, and completion states are stored only on the user's device. Android may include this local app data in system backup or device transfer when the user enables those operating-system features.

Data collection and sharing

NudgeNest does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data. The app requests no network, location, camera, microphone, contacts, advertising, or notification permissions. It contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Local checklist data remains on the device until the user edits or deletes it. Users can remove all NudgeNest data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

NudgeNest is a general productivity tool and is not directed specifically to children. Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Keeping data on-device reduces exposure to remote services. No method of local storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, and users should protect access to their device.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's practices change. The revised policy will show a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact displayed on the NudgeNest Google Play listing.
